03 / Joint model

Two Hull–White factors and lognormal FX.

All dynamics are simulated jointly under the USD money-market measure with EURUSD quoted as USD per EUR.

Domestic-measure dynamics

The negative EUR quanto term is required by the measure and FX quotation. The log-FX update includes the Itô correction.

USD Hull–White dru=[θu(t)−auru]dt+σudWu
EUR Hull–White under the USD measure dre=[θe(t)−aere−ρeSσeσS(t)]dt+σedWe
EURUSD level process dSS=(ru−re)dt+σS(t)dWS
Log-EURUSD process dlogS=[ru−re−12σS2(t)]dt+σS(t)dWS

05 / Bermudan exercise

Backward regression, forward stopping.

The holder cancels the remaining swap only when its estimated cancellable continuation value is negative beyond the configured tolerance.

Decision rule

Common cash flows due at the event date are excluded from the comparison, paid, and then retained regardless of the decision.

Conditional continuationCi(Xi)=EUSD[future cancellable cash flows+Vi+1|Xi]
Zero-fee cancellationCancel if0>C^i(Xi)+εexercise
Deterministic tie ruleContinue if|C^i(Xi)|≤εexercise

Regression state includes standardized USD/EUR rate factors, log FX, conditional PV features, squares, and cross terms. The frozen policy is applied to an independent pricing sample.

07 / Implementation & local use

QuantLib where native; explicit hybrid logic where required.

The Flask page is observational. The standalone engine remains the source of pricing results and the MCP server remains the machine-to-machine interface.

QuantLib responsibilities

  • Calendars, schedules, day counts, OIS helpers, yield curves, and discount factors.
  • ql.HullWhite, ql.SwaptionHelper, and ql.JamshidianSwaptionEngine for rate calibration.
  • Black-vol quote conventions and analytic building blocks used by calibration.
  • Reproducible curve and helper repricing diagnostics.

Explicit Python responsibilities

  • Joint USD-measure transition with the EUR quanto drift and stochastic-rate FX drift.
  • Exact OU kernels, interval covariance integration, and correlated antithetic paths.
  • Pathwise cross-currency cash flows, notional exchanges, discounting, and FX conversion.
  • Two-sample LSM cancellation policy, exercise accounting, confidence intervals, and martingale tests.
